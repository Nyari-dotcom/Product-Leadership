## Refine and complete your charter
- **What We Own. What this team owns.:** 1. Core customer value

- The end-to-end experience of helping users recognise changes in their wellbeing and take meaningful action.
- The quality and coherence of the core Fable experience across the customer journey.
- The product outcomes associated with meaningful action and wellbeing value.

2. Activation and first value

- The experience that gets a new user from entering Fable to understanding their wellbeing and taking their first meaningful action.
- Reducing friction that prevents users from reaching first value.
- Onboarding decisions where they directly affect the core value proposition.

3. Engagement and retention

- The mechanisms that help users return to Fable and continue taking meaningful wellness actions.
- Retention experiments where they support the core customer-value loop.
- Understanding whether engagement represents meaningful behaviour rather than vanity metrics such as opens or clicks.

4. AI-enabled experiences
- The product experience and customer outcome of AI-powered check-ins and recommendations.
- How AI contributes to the Notice → Understand → Act loop.
- Ensuring AI experiences remain within Fable's strategic and clinical boundaries.

5. Product learning

The experimentation agenda required to validate Fable's strategic bets.
Measuring whether product changes improve meaningful action, wellbeing outcomes, retention and willingness to pay.
Sharing evidence that informs future roadmap decisions.
- **What is out of scope.:** - Clinical diagnosis, assessment or treatment.
- Clinical policy or professional clinical standards.
- Marketing acquisition strategy or channel performance.
- Growth campaigns, paid acquisition or lifecycle marketing execution.
- External partnerships owned by another function.
- Commercial pricing decisions without the relevant commercial/Growth stakeholders.
- Organisation-wide data, privacy or regulatory policy.
- Individual PM roadmaps when those roadmaps optimise against outcomes that conflict with the Fable strategy.
- **Cross-boundary decisions that need a joint call.:** - Changes the end-to-end customer journey across more than one PM's area.
- Changes the AI check-in experience in a way that affects onboarding or retention.
- Trades off acquisition against product experience or retention.
- Introduces a clinical, safety, privacy or regulatory implication.
- Changes a shared customer metric or its definition.
- Creates a material conflict between a local team metric and Fable's strategic outcome.
- **How We Decide. Who decides feature and scope calls.:** 1. Start with the customer outcome

Every significant product decision must answer:

Which Fable outcome are we trying to change, for which user, and what evidence would tell us we were right?

Teams should prioritise against the Fable OKRs rather than optimise their own surface metrics independently.

For example:

“Increase onboarding completion” is not sufficient.

The stronger question is:

“Will reducing onboarding friction increase the number of new users who reach meaningful action within seven days?”

2. The directly responsible PM decides within their boundary

The PM who owns the relevant customer journey or product area has decision authority when:

The decision stays within their defined boundary.
It does not materially affect another team's customer journey.
It does not create a material safety, clinical, privacy or regulatory risk.
It is consistent with the Fable strategy and agreed OKRs.

Default: the closest accountable owner decides.

We do not escalate decisions simply because another team has an opinion.

3. Cross-boundary decisions require a joint call

When a decision materially affects multiple teams, the relevant owners make the decision together.

For example:

PM 3 wants to change the AI check-in → affects onboarding → affects PM 1 → potentially affects retention → affects PM 2.

This is not “PM 3's feature decision.”

It is a shared customer-journey decision.

The involved PMs should align on:

customer outcome
strategic bet
expected trade-offs
success metric
experiment design
decision deadline

The team closest to the primary customer outcome should lead the decision, while affected teams have explicit input.

4. Growth does not get a separate optimisation lane

Growth is a partner in Fable's customer-value loop, not an independent optimisation function.

Growth may optimise acquisition, conversion and monetisation, but initiatives must be assessed against downstream product outcomes.

For example:

Acquisition increases 30%, but six-month retention falls significantly.

This is not automatically a Growth success.

The teams jointly assess the quality and economic value of the acquired customers.

The shared question is:
Are we acquiring customers who experience Fable's value?
- **How cross-team conflicts escalate.:** Step 1 — Resolve at the working level

Who: Relevant PMs / Growth lead
How: 30-minute decision discussion using customer outcome, strategy and evidence.
Timeframe: Within 2 business days.

The disagreement must be framed as a decision, not a debate:

“We disagree about X because we believe A will produce outcome Y, while B will produce outcome Z.”

Step 2 — Escalate to Fable Product Lead

If unresolved:

Who: Fable Product Lead / Product Director
How: Each side presents its recommendation, evidence, risks and expected impact on the Fable OKRs.
Timeframe: Decision within 2 business days.

The Product Lead makes the call based on:

Customer outcome
Strategic alignment
Evidence
Safety/clinical implications
Impact across the end-to-end journey
Reversibility of the decision
Step 3 — Executive escalation

Escalate beyond Product only when the conflict involves:

material clinical or safety risk
regulatory/privacy risk
significant commercial commitment
major change to Fable's strategy
substantial resource or organisational trade-off

Who: Appropriate executive sponsor / leadership forum
Timeframe: Decision within 5 business days.
- **Who resolves escalations from outside the team.:** Who: Appropriate executive sponsor / leadership forum
Timeframe: Decision within 5 business days.
