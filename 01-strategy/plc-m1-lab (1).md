# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Help everyday adults recognise when their mental wellbeing is starting to decline and take the right action before it becomes a bigger problem. It's also to help adults maintain their mental wellbeing.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Adults 18+ who are generally functioning in their everyday lives but want to proactively manage their mental wellbeing. We'll be starting with the South African market before we expand to other regions and countries.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Fable wins by connecting them into a proactive mental-wellness loop:

Notice → Understand → Act → Measure → Adapt → Escalate when necessary

Notice: Help users recognise meaningful changes in their wellbeing through regular check-ins and behavioural signals.
Understand: Explain what those changes may mean in accessible, non-diagnostic language.
Act: Recommend personalised, evidence-informed actions such as exercise, sleep improvements, social connection, self-care activities or professional support.
Measure: Show whether those actions are helping over time.
Adapt: Personalise recommendations based on what the user actually does and what appears to help.
Escalate: Connect users with qualified mental-health professionals when professional support is appropriate.
Potential moat

Over time, Fable can build a proprietary wellness-behaviour and intervention dataset showing which interventions work for which types of users and circumstances.

The defensibility therefore comes less from owning “clinical data” and more from the relationship between user signals, interventions, engagement and outcomes generated through Fable.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** - Under-18s
- Users requiring emergency or inpatient psychiatric care
- AI-led diagnosis or treatment

We will not be using AI to diagnose patients. This can only be done by mental wellness professionals. Fable cannot reposition as a clinical mental-health platform, for regulatory and liability reasons.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Objective 1: Prove that Fable creates meaningful everyday mental-wellness value.

Objective 2: Establish Fable as a product users return to proactively manage their wellbeing.

Objective 3: Prove a sustainable willingness to pay.
- **KR1:** Objective 1: At least 50% of active users demonstrate sustained improvement or maintenance in their chosen wellbeing measure within six months.
Objective 2: Achieve 50% six-month retention among new users acquired in the defined Q2 cohort.
Objective 3: Increase the percentage of active users converting to paid from 20% to 35% by Q4.
- **KR2:** Objective 1: At least 60% of users who identify a meaningful decline complete at least one recommended action within 7 days.
Objective 2: At least 40% of retained users complete a meaningful wellness action or check-in each month.
Objective 3: Achieve 70%+ three-month retention among paying customers.
- **KR3:** Objective 1: At least 70% of users report that Fable helped them better understand and take action on their mental wellbeing.
Objective 2: Reduce monthly user churn by X% from the Q1 baseline by Q4.
Objective 3: Demonstrate that paying customers who use Fable's core intervention loop have materially higher retention than paying customers who do not.
